Store policies

How ordering, paying, mailing and returns work at Crazed TCG. Last updated October 2026.

Shipping policy

Tracked Mailing

Tracked Mailing is a flat fee per order, no matter how many cards you buy. We mail your order once your payment is confirmed, and you can follow its status in your account.

Self-pickup

Self-pickup is free. It's available at a listed trade show, or for orders of $50 or more. Leave your preferred time and place in the order note, and we'll confirm it with you on Telegram.

Packaging

Every card is packed to protect it in transit.

Returns & refunds

Each card is a single collectible, so all sales are final once payment is made. We'll make it right if something goes wrong:

  • Card not as described. If you receive the wrong card, or its condition is clearly worse than the grade listed, message us within 7 days of receiving it, with clear photos.
  • Damaged in transit. Message us within 7 days with photos of the card and the packaging. An unboxing photo or video helps.
  • Lost parcel. If tracking shows no progress, message us and we'll follow up with the courier.

Once we've checked your photos, we'll offer a refund or a replacement where one is available. We reply within 1–2 business days.

Terms of sale

Prices and payment

All prices are in Singapore dollars. We take payment by PayNow only. After you place an order, pay the total shown, then send your payment screenshot and order ID to us on Telegram.

Reserved cards

Your cards are reserved for you when you check out. If an order isn't paid, we may cancel it and return the cards to the store.

Card condition

Photos are reference images. Each listing states the card's actual condition: NM (Near Mint), LP (Lightly Played), MP (Moderately Played) or HP (Heavily Played). Ask us for extra photos before you order if you need them.

Telegram claim sales

In our claim sale channel, replying "claim" on a card means you're buying it at the listed price. We send your invoice on Telegram, and your claimed cards show up in your account.

Contact us

Questions about an order? Message us on Telegram at our order contact, and include your order ID if you have one.
